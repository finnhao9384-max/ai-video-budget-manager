TITLE: THE RIGHT FREQUENCY  
GENRE: Sci-Fi / Drama / Surreal Commercial  
LOGLINE: Trapped in a conformist, judgmental metropolis, a lonely man with a television for a head short-circuits in despair, only to discover a utopian haven where unique minds truly belong.  
SCENE 1: INT. DILAPIDATED BATHROOM - MORNING  
A cold, dim bathroom lined with pale greenish ceramic tiles. Water pipes hum in the gloom.  
In front of a smudged mirror stands a shirtless MAN, wrapped only in a white towel around his waist.  
Where his neck should meet a human head rests a heavy, vintage cathode-ray tube (CRT) television monitor. The glass screen glows with blinding, crackling white static noise.  
The TV-head man lifts his right hand to the grooved dial on the side of his monitor. He twists the dial with deliberate, trembling clicks.  
INSERT - CRT SCREEN  
A harsh pulse of static flickers and steadies for a split second. In lowercase serif font, a single word surfaces on the white phosphor screen:  
frequency.  
  
The signal immediately dissolves back into fuzzy, deafening noise.  
SCENE 2: INT. SUBWAY CAR - DAY  
The TV-head man now wears a tailored grey business suit, white shirt, and a dark tie.  
He stands in the center aisle of a dimly lit, rattling subway train, clutching a stainless steel pole. His screen illuminates the cramped car with rhythmic bursts of white static.  
Around him, rows of ordinary commuters in heavy winter coats stare at him with quiet suspicion, cold stares, and discomfort.  
A WOMAN seated across from him leans toward her companion, eyes locked onto the screen.  
WOMAN  
(whispering)  
Did you see that?  
The TV-head man remains frozen in place as commuters exchange unsettled glances.  
SCENE 3: INT. SUBWAY STATION & ESCALATOR - CONTINUOUS  
The train doors slide open with a dull pneumatic hiss. Commuters stream out onto the platform. The TV-head man follows behind, his screen casting cold reflections onto the tile floor.  
He steps onto a long, cavernous escalator descending through a curved tiled tunnel. Flocks of commuters on adjacent escalators turn their heads in unison, staring at his electronic visage.  
At the concourse, he approaches the automated turnstile gates, inserts his transit card, and steps through into the public exit.  
SCENE 4: EXT. METROPOLITAN STREET - DAY  
The TV-head man emerges onto a wide city street beneath a pale overcast sky.  
Scores of business commuters in dark suits march past him in hurried sync. Many carry identical white takeout plastic bags.  
From high above, the crowd parts around the TV-head man like water around a stone. He is completely isolated in the middle of a sea of humanity.  
SCENE 5: INT. CORPORATE OPEN-PLAN OFFICE - DAY  
Rows of gray cubicles span an open floor bathed in harsh fluorescent light.  
The TV-head man sits alone at his terminal, clacking away on a keyboard while data scrolls across his desktop monitor. His television head hums with frantic, unresolved static.  
In adjacent cubicles, coworkers huddle around screens, murmuring and side-eyeing him.  
COWORKER 1  
(whispering)  
Did you see the screen?  
A male manager in a dark suit pauses beside his partition, glaring down at him with cold disapproval before walking away.  
Across the aisle, three colleagues lean in close over an open terminal.  
COWORKER 2  
Did you see the reports on him?  
COWORKER 3  
Scary.  
The TV-head man keeps typing, but the static across his screen intensifies, buzzing violently until the display cuts dead black.  
SCENE 6: EXT. CITY STREET & CONVENIENCE STORE - NIGHT  
Rain puddles glisten on the asphalt outside a glowing 7-Eleven storefront.  
A group of local men laugh together outside, holding warm coffee cups. As the TV-head man trudges past in the dark, their laughter subsides into uneasy silence.  
His dress shoes splash through puddles, reflecting neon signs and street lamps.  
SCENE 7: EXT. PARKING LOT / CONVENIENCE STORE - CONTINUOUS  
Exhausted, the TV-head man sinks onto the concrete curb stop in front of the convenience store. He clutches an opened aluminum beer can in his hand.  
His screen buzzes with dull, frantic white noise.  
Slowly, he raises the open beer can above his monitor. With deliberate desperation, he pours the beer directly into the ventilation grates atop his television head.  
Violent electrical arcs crackle! Sparks explode from the chassis in bright orange showers! Thick smoke billows from the casing as distorted scan lines violently tear across the monitor!  
Bystanders recoil in terror, gasping.  
Overwhelmed by blinding sparks and agony, the TV-head man leaps to his feet and bolts into the dark parking lot!  
SCENE 8: EXT. DESOLATE LOT / UTILITY POLE - NIGHT  
Sparks spray from his sparking monitor as he sprints through the dark. He trips over his own feet, crashing hard against the wet asphalt beneath a single streetlamp.  
He lies prone, trembling, smoke rising into the cold air.  
A heavy, industrial power cable lies coiled across the pavement nearby, rooted to the base of a tall wooden pole.  
Reaching out with scraped hands, he crawls across the wet pavement toward the cable. He takes hold of the thick rubber cord with both hands, pulling it tight against his chest.  
SCENE 9: EXT. BILLBOARD - NIGHT  
The camera tilts up along the tall wooden utility pole into the night sky.  
Floodlights snap on with a loud buzz, illuminating a towering billboard:  
do you feel lost?  
  
ALTER CITY  
  
a place for people that build different.  
  
alter.city  
  
SCENE 10: INT. GRAND TRAIN STATION - DAY  
A black wheeled suitcase glides smoothly across the polished stone floor of a sun-drenched railway terminal.  
The TV-head man strides purposefully forward. He has discarded his suit; he now wears a comfortable olive-green hoodie, dark denim, and casual sneakers.  
He pauses at the open-air train platform, breathing in the fresh air.  
SCENE 11: INT. COMMUTER TRAIN - DAY  
Sunlight pours through panoramic train windows.  
The TV-head man sits comfortably in a plush booth seat, looking outside as lush landscapes and contemporary buildings glide past.  
The static across his screen has softened into a gentle, calm white wash.  
SCENE 12: EXT. ALTER CITY STATION PLATFORM - DAY  
The modern train glides to a stop alongside a wide, sunlit platform labeled "DEEP AREA 001 STATION". Across the horizon stands Alter City—a utopian metropolis of crystalline towers and organic architecture.  
The train doors open. The TV-head man steps onto the platform with his suitcase.  
He stops in awe.  
The commuters around him are entirely extraordinary:  
* A woman in an elegant trench coat carries a birdcage as her head.  
* Another commuter sports a gigantic, blossoming pink peony flower head.  
* Other travelers walk by with cardboard box heads, vintage speaker heads, and leather trunk heads.  
Nobody stares; nobody whispers. Everyone moves in peaceful harmony.  
The TV-head man smiles beneath his screen and sits down on a sunny wooden platform bench, setting his suitcase beside his legs.  
SCENE 13: EXT. STATION BENCH & TERRACE - CONTINUOUS  
In the terrace behind him, other inhabitants gather:  
* A figure crowned with bare winter tree branches reads by a table.  
* An intellectual with a golden honeycomb hive head chats with a companion sporting a crescent moon head.  
* An older gentleman with a classic analog clock face for a head reads a broadsheet newspaper.  
Beside our protagonist, a gentleman in a brown suit with a head made entirely of fresh, multicolored autumn flowers takes a seat on the bench and calmly opens his newspaper.  
A moment later, an elegant man in a sharp black suit with an antique accordion bellows camera for a head approaches and sits on his other side.  
The three sit side by side: Camera Head, TV Head, and Flower Head.  
A quiet, profound sense of belonging fills the frame.  
SCENE 14: EXT. BENCH - CLOSE-UP ON TV HEAD  
CAMERA PUSHES IN on the protagonist's CRT screen.  
The chaotic static softens, glowing with warm amber light.  
From the center of the phosphor tube, glowing pixel blocks arrange themselves into a bright, cheerful SMILEY FACE :).
