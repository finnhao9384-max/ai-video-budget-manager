**Alternative: Bundled Multi-Shot Generation**  
Instead of 9 separate minimum-duration tasks, all 9 shots could be attempted as one continuous 11-second Seedance 2.5 multi-shot generation (within its 4–30s range), avoiding the 4s-per-shot minimum waste entirely:  
* 480P: 11s × 20cr/s = 220 credits (video only, single round)  
* 720P: 11s × 40cr/s = 440 credits  
This is far cheaper, but it's a materially different plan with real risk: one continuous generation has to carry a stadium wide shot, a helmet close-up, a prop transformation, a true split-screen, a particle explosion, and a static logo lockup — six very different visual treatments — in one pass, with no ability to retry a single failing shot without regenerating the whole take.
