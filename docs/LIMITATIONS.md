# Limitations and Responsible Use

- TapNow executes the Skill; the host model, temperature and exact invocation environment were not recorded in the test exports. Repeated runs may differ.
- The offline checker does not automatically receive Agent output, enforce a production cap, or repair the Skill's runtime arithmetic.
- All prices are author-collected price records dated 2026-09-26. They do not establish current prices, account access, or actual settlement.
- Mode labels reflect recorded pricing data. A first/last-frame label alone does not prove that a last frame is mandatory or that every single-frame configuration is accepted.
- Three detailed-storyboard transcripts are a small convenience sample. No measured no-Skill baseline, independent quality benchmark, or repeated-run reliability result is available.
- Quality recommendations reflect the author's creative experience and understanding of community perceptions about model strengths. The project does not document specific community sources or controlled comparisons of motion, text, fidelity or identity consistency. These recommendations are experience-based judgments, not measured quality rankings.
- A lower unit price does not necessarily mean a lower cost per usable result. The Commercial comparison does not quantify model-specific retry needs, so the author's preference for Seedance 2.0 cannot by itself establish a cost advantage over H3.
- MiniMax H3 and H3 Max have different supported settings in the pricing snapshot: H3 has no 480P option, while H3 Max does. Family-level wording in the Right Frequency output obscures this distinction.
- Shot counts can legitimately change during production. The AURA detail-to-summary discrepancy concerns an unreconciled count within one response, not a difference between the original plan and later production.
- Higher resolution does not prove better visual quality. Nominal resolution comparisons are not quality-equivalence tests.
- Attempt allowances are assumptions. Some assets may succeed immediately; others may fail repeatedly. Rejected but successfully generated outputs can still consume credits.
- Actual production changed scope. Its 3,507-credit author-recorded result is not a measured saving or a forecast-accuracy score. The dataset does not include per-operation settlement evidence.
- A promotional expiry has no stated timezone. The checker conservatively rejects the expiry date as ambiguous and later dates as expired; the Skill can describe conditional estimates.
- Agent usage, audio, editing, enhancement and cancellation fees may be unquoted. A quoted generation plan fitting a balance does not guarantee the entire production fits.
- Only use scripts and assets you are authorized to submit to the host. Review private data and source-material permissions before sharing a repository. Brand names in test examples do not imply endorsement or permission.
- The transcripts preserve the observed outputs, including their errors. The release Skill has not been revised to address these evaluation findings; resolution requires further development and testing.
